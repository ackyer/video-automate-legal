Summary

  • Video Automate is a personal-use tool. Its owner uses it with their own accounts, and it has no external users.
  • It uses YouTube API Services and the Instagram and TikTok APIs to publish the owner’s videos and read their metrics.
  • Access keys and metrics are stored on the owner’s computer. Keys only leave it to go to the platform that issued them.
  • Data is not sold. To analyze them, some Instagram and TikTok performance figures are sent to the artificial intelligence services the owner uses (Claude and Gemini). Data from YouTube API Services is not sent to any of them.
  • Access can be revoked at any time and the data is deleted within a maximum of 30 days.

Controller and acceptance

The data controller is Ander Akier Ayucar Chasco, the owner of the Video Automate tool (“the owner”). Contact: akier.empresa@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the data Video Automate uses when a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account is connected. By connecting an account to Video Automate, you accept this policy.

YouTube API Services

Video Automate uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Data from the Google account is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is used

Video Automate only accesses the platforms whose accounts the owner has connected, and only with the permissions the owner accepts on each platform’s authorization screen. It is always data from the owner’s own accounts and videos: the tool does not access other people’s accounts and reads only aggregate figures for each video (for example, how many comments it has), not the content of comments or viewers’ personal data.

YouTube

  • Permissions: upload videos to the channel (youtube.upload), read the channel’s data and statistics and those of its videos (youtube.readonly), and view YouTube Analytics reports for the channel’s content (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Channel data: channel identifier and name.
  • Own videos: the videos the tool publishes to the channel: identifier, title, description, duration and publication status.
  • Metrics: views, likes, number of comments, watch time and retention, and subscribers gained.

Although Google’s read permission is broader, the tool only uses the data described here.

Instagram

  • Permissions (Instagram API with Instagram Login, professional account): basic account data (instagram_business_basic), publish Reels (instagram_business_content_publish) and read metrics (instagram_business_manage_insights).
  • Account data: identifier, username and number of followers.
  • Own videos: the Reels the tool publishes: identifier, caption, date and link.
  • Metrics: views, reach, average watch time, percentage of viewers who skip the video at the start, likes, number of comments, shares and saves.

TikTok

  • Permissions (official TikTok APIs: Login Kit, Content Posting API and Display API): basic profile (user.info.basic), publish videos or send them as drafts (video.publish, video.upload) and list the owner’s own videos with their figures (video.list).
  • Account data: identifier and display name of the profile.
  • Own videos: the videos the tool publishes: identifier, description, duration and link.
  • Metrics: views, likes, comments and shares (figures).

What the data is used for

Only for two purposes:

  1. Publishing the owner’s videos to their own accounts.
  2. Measuring how those videos perform in order to improve the next videos.

The data is not used for advertising, for building profiles of people, or for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Access keys (the codes each platform gives the tool when the owner authorizes it) are stored only on the owner’s computer, and are not sent to anyone except the platform that issued them, when the tool calls its API. Metrics are stored on the owner’s computer, in files and in a local database. The tool has no server of its own.

How long it is kept

  • YouTube API data: at most 30 calendar days without being checked. After that, the tool queries YouTube again, checking that the owner’s authorization is still valid and that the video has not been deleted, or deletes the data.
  • Instagram and TikTok data: while the account remains connected and the data is needed to compare videos and decide the next ones. It is deleted within a maximum of 30 days after access is revoked.
  • Access keys: while the account remains connected. When access is revoked they stop working and are deleted.

Who it is shared with

Data is not sold, rented or transferred, and it is not used for advertising.

To analyze how the videos perform, the tool sends performance figures for the owner’s Instagram and TikTok videos (for example, views or retention) to the artificial intelligence services the owner works with: Claude, by Anthropic, and Gemini, by Google. Those services process them on the owner’s behalf and under their own terms. Access keys and viewers’ personal data are never sent to them.

Data obtained through YouTube API Services is not sent to any artificial intelligence service or any third party: it is processed only on the owner’s computer.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time, on each platform. Once revoked, Video Automate can no longer publish or read data for that account. On the platforms’ permission screens the application appears under the name “video-automate”.

Google (YouTube)

Open the permissions page of your Google account, select “video-automate” and remove its access.

Instagram

On instagram.com: More › Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites › Active › Remove, next to “video-automate”. Menus are shown in the language of the account and their names may vary; see the Instagram Help Center.

TikTok

In the TikTok app: Profile › menu › Settings and privacy › Security & permissions › Apps and services permissions, and remove “video-automate”. Menu names may vary; see the TikTok Help Center.

Data deletion

When access to an account is revoked, Video Automate deletes the data it holds for that account (access keys, account data, video information and metrics) as soon as possible and, in any case, within a maximum of 30 calendar days of the revocation. To do this, it periodically checks that each authorization is still valid and deletes the data of accounts whose authorization it can no longer renew.

Deletion of the data can also be requested by writing to akier.empresa@gmail.com: it is handled within a maximum of 7 calendar days.

Revoking access does not remove videos that are already published on the platforms: they remain on the owner’s accounts and are managed from each platform.
