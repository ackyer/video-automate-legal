Video Automate

A personal-use tool that publishes AI-generated vertical videos to its owner’s own accounts and reads their metrics to improve the next videos.

What it is

Video Automate is a personal-use software tool. It generates vertical videos with artificial intelligence and publishes them to its owner’s own accounts on YouTube (Shorts), Instagram (Reels) and TikTok. It then reads the metrics of those videos, such as views, retention and followers gained, to decide better which videos to make next.

It uses YouTube API Services and the official Instagram and TikTok APIs, always with the owner’s authorization.

Who uses it

It is used by a single person: its owner, Ander Akier Ayucar Chasco, with their own accounts. It has no external users: there is no sign-up, there are no user accounts and the tool is not offered to third parties.

This website exists because the platforms require a public page with the privacy policy and the terms of the applications that use their APIs.

What it does and does not do

  • It publishes videos only to the owner’s accounts. It does not publish to other people’s accounts.
  • It reads only statistics of the owner’s own videos, such as views or retention. It does not read viewers’ personal data.
  • It stores access keys and metrics on the owner’s computer.
  • It does not sell data. It only sends performance figures for the videos to the owner’s artificial intelligence services, to analyze them. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is not sent to any artificial intelligence service.
  • Videos carry the AI-generated content label wherever the platform offers one.
  • This website uses no cookies, scripts or analytics.
